fetal-echo.org

Clinical decision-support aid — not a diagnostic test. Z-scores are derived from published reference equations valid within the gestational-age ranges of each source. Cardiac function in FGR, TTTS and placental disease should be interpreted in the context of serial measurements, Doppler staging, growth biometry, and the overall clinical picture.
Designed for: FGR · TTTS · placental disease. This tool focuses on the parameters most relevant to fetal cardiac adaptation under placental stress — Doppler peak velocities and inflow E/A ratios (Gagnon 2016), aortic isthmus indices, RV fractional area change, ventricular filling and ejection time fractions, and cardiac output. For structural dimensions and arch Z-scores, use the main Fetal Z-Score Calculator.

Biometric inputs

Doppler velocities & inflow E/A — Gagnon 2016

Pulsed-wave Doppler peak velocities (m/s) and E/A ratios, Z-scored by gestational age (Gagnon 2016, validated 18–39 weeks). Fetal inflow is A-wave dominant (E/A < 1); E/A rises gradually with gestation as ventricular compliance improves — elevation is a sign of ventricular dysfunction in FGR and TTTS.

Aortic isthmus indices

Isthmus systolic VTI (m), diastolic antegrade VTI (m), and isthmic flow index (IFI = (systolic+diastolic)/systolic). Negative or absent diastolic flow indicates elevated right-sided afterload — a key finding in FGR.

RV fractional area change

Enter RV end-diastolic area (EDA) and end-systolic area (ESA) from the 4-chamber view. FAC = (EDA − ESA) / EDA × 100. Z-scored by gestational age (Luewan 2011) and by estimated fetal weight (Messing 2011). FAC < 30–35% in the context of FGR or TTTS suggests significant systolic dysfunction.

Filling & ejection time fractions

Measure filling time (valve opening to closure, diastole) and ejection time from PW Doppler; cycle length = RR interval (ms). FTF = filling/cycle × 100, ETF = ejection/cycle × 100. Shortened filling fraction is an early sign of diastolic dysfunction in FGR. Reference: Carvalho 2017 (filling), Carvalho 2002 (ejection).

Cardiac output

SV = π/4 × D² × VTI (cm³); CO = SV × HR (mL/min). Combined cardiac output is indexed to estimated fetal weight. Reference range for indexed combined CO: 425–550 mL/min/kg (Mielke 2001). Increased CO is seen in fetal anaemia and early FGR compensation; decreased CO reflects late decompensation.

Ductus arteriosus PI — fixed reference

Non-GA-dependent fixed reference (Tulzer 1991): mean 2.46, normal range 1.94–2.98 (mean ± 2SD). Low PI (ductal constriction) occurs with maternal NSAIDs; high PI occurs with tocolytics (terbutaline) or duct-dependent systemic circulation (e.g. HLHS). This index deliberately does not enter the Z-score findings.

Ductal PI unitless · (peak − end-diastolic) / mean velocity
