Reference range: Zidere V et al., J Am Soc Echocardiogr 2021 (validated 13–36 weeks).

Biometry

Gestational age is required for all Z-scores. Valid range: 13–36 weeks.

Outflow tract peak velocities

Pulsed-wave Doppler, cm/s. Measure at valve level; angle-correct to <20°.

Left inflow — mitral valve

E-wave and A-wave in cm/s; E/A ratio (computed or entered directly).
Fetal inflow is A-wave dominant (E/A < 1) throughout gestation — opposite to the adult heart. E/A approaching 1 is abnormal and warrants investigation.

Right inflow — tricuspid valve

E-wave and A-wave in cm/s; E/A ratio (computed or entered directly).
Same principle as the mitral valve — E/A is normally < 1. Tricuspid E/A approaching 1 may indicate RV diastolic dysfunction or volume load.

Fetal heart rate

Average rate in bpm from M-mode or Doppler.
